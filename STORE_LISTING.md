## Short description (max 80 chars)

> A precision VPN client for VLESS, VMess, Trojan, and Shadowsocks.

## Full description (max 4000 chars)

> Ai V2Ray Client is a precision-instrument-style VPN utility that operates
> entirely against servers YOU configure. No accounts. No telemetry. No
> advertisers. No remote log collection.
>
> Features
> • VLESS, VMess, Trojan, Shadowsocks
> • Tap-to-connect tun-based tunnel with kill switch
> • QR scan to import server configurations
> • Multiple profiles, per-profile server selection
> • Latency and live throughput gauges
> • Brushed-steel industrial UI
>
> How it works
> The app establishes a VpnService tun on your Android device and forwards
> your selected traffic through the Xray core using your own configuration
> JSON. All credentials and tunnel parameters are kept locally on your
> device.
>
> Privacy
> This app does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data. It contains
> no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs, and no crash reporting. The full
> privacy policy is available in-app and at the store-listing URL.
>
> Permissions
> • INTERNET — for the VPN tunnel
> • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — best-effort reachability check
> • FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE (vpn_tunnel) — typed FGS as required by Android 14+
> • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — Android 13+ notification consent
>
> Compliance
> Ai V2Ray Client makes a prominent in-app disclosure as required by Play's
> VpnService policy and uses only the permissions strictly required to operate
> a VPN tunnel. It does not block advertising traffic unless you configure it
> to.
